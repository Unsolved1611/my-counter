Mindful Tools

Urge Surfing

Urges are like waves. They rise, peak, and inevitably subside. Instead of fighting it, breathe deeply, observe the feeling without judgment, and "ride" it until it dissolves.

The HALT Rule

Most cravings strike when you are Hungry, Angry, Lonely, or Tired. When an urge rises, check in with your physical needs first.

The 10-Minute Pause

Tell yourself: "I can choose to engage, but I will wait exactly 10 minutes first." Drink a glass of water or walk outside. Usually, the urge passes.
